# JOYNURE Brand Foundation

Status: approved visual direction, production asset milestone.

## Brand idea

JOYNURE combines **joy** and **nurture**. The identity is intentionally broader than a baby, pet, or fitness symbol so it can support all three product categories without making the store feel fragmented.

The mark combines:

- a geometric `J`;
- a smile / embracing curve for care;
- a coral dot for joy and human warmth;
- a small golden leaf for gentle growth.

## Production palette

| Token | Hex | Primary use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nurture teal | `#1F5C55` | Primary buttons, links, logo, key UI controls |
| Joy coral | `#FF7A68` | Promotional badges, selected highlights, secondary accents |
| Warm cream | `#FFF8F0` | Main page background and calm surfaces |
| Golden sand | `#F2C66D` | Small highlights, icons, decorative details |
| Deep charcoal | `#24312E` | Body text, headings, dark surfaces |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text and marks on dark backgrounds |

## Usage balance

- Warm cream: 60–70% of the storefront.
- Deep charcoal and teal: 20–30% for typography and conversion controls.
- Coral and golden sand: no more than 10% combined, used as purposeful accents.
- Never place coral body text on cream or white; reserve it for larger UI accents with verified contrast.

## Typography direction

- Headings and wordmark direction: rounded geometric sans serif, confident but friendly.
- Storefront recommendation: use Shopify-hosted **Inter** for both headings and body in the first implementation milestone to avoid an extra font dependency and keep performance predictable.
- Headings: 700 weight; body: 400; labels and navigation: 600.
- Avoid playful handwritten fonts, luxury serifs, and childlike bubble lettering.

## Logo files

- `assets/joynure-logo-primary.svg`: full-color horizontal logo for cream or white backgrounds.
- `assets/joynure-logo-dark.svg`: one-color charcoal logo for light backgrounds and print.
- `assets/joynure-logo-white.svg`: one-color white logo for teal, charcoal, or photography overlays.
- `assets/joynure-mark.svg`: standalone full-color mark.
- `assets/joynure-favicon.svg`: small-size optimized favicon with teal tile.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space around the horizontal logo equal to at least the coral dot diameter.
- Minimum digital width for the horizontal logo: 140 px.
- Use the standalone mark below 140 px or when the available area is square.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual parts, add shadows, or place the full-color mark on visually busy photography.

## Shopify mapping for the next milestone

- Page background: warm cream.
- Main text: deep charcoal.
- Primary button: nurture teal with white text.
- Primary button hover: deep charcoal with white text.
- Secondary button: transparent with teal border and teal text.
- Sale or campaign badge: joy coral with deep charcoal text.
- Focus ring: nurture teal with a cream separation outline.
- Card surfaces: white or a very light cream tint with subtle charcoal/10% borders.

The brand package does not change products, prices, variants, inventory, cart behavior, markets, translations, SEO, analytics, or Shopify theme settings by itself.
